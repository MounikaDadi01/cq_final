# ATLAS TOOLING — Brand Guidelines v4

## Colours

Our palette is expressed in RGB because our print vendor requires it.

- Primary: rgb(30, 41, 59)
- Secondary: rgb(71, 85, 105)
- Accent: rgb(220, 38, 38)
- Surface: rgb(248, 250, 252)
- Ink: rgb(15, 23, 42)

## Typography

- heading: Satoshi Display
- body: Satoshi

## Type scale

- h1: 3.25rem
- h2: 2.5rem
- h3: 1.5rem
- body: 1.0625rem
- caption: 0.8125rem

## Shape

- border-radius: 2px

## Applying it

Headlines are Satoshi Display Bold. An h1 is 52px on a square and may drop to
44px on a banner — use judgement. Accent red is for the call to action and for
error states only.

A primary button is a solid fill of accent red with surface label text.
