# Northwind Foods

A cold-chain logistics company for fresh produce. Sells to grocery buyers and
distribution managers. Sober, precise, never playful about food safety.

## Palette

- primary: #14532D
- secondary: #4D7C0F
- accent: #F59E0B
- surface: #FFFFFF
- ink: #14181A
- muted: #6B7280

## Type

- heading: Barlow
- body: Barlow

## Type scale

- h1: 46px
- h2: 34px
- h3: 22px
- body: 17px
- caption: 13px

## Shape

- border-radius: 6px
- Cards and image containers use the 6px corner. Buttons use the same.

## Applying it

Headline type is Barlow 700, set tight. An h1 is 46px on every canvas. If the
headline does not fit at 46, cut the copy; do not scale the type. Body copy is
Barlow 400, and the eyebrow above a headline is Barlow 600 at caption size,
letterspaced, in the accent.

The accent carries the call to action and almost nothing else. A primary button
is a solid fill of #F59E0B with ink label text at Barlow 600. Secondary buttons
are a 1px #F59E0B outline on surface with the label in the same amber. Never
tint the accent, and never use it as a background for a region larger than a
button.

Photography is refrigerated warehouses, pallets and produce in cold light.
Never a person holding food. Never a plated meal.
